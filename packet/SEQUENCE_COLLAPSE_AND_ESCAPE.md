# Sequence Design — Laptoon Collapse and Water Escape

**Status:** current preferred transition from city drama into the migration journey

This sequence should remain clear and fast. It does not need extra rescue dependants, a fake father, a prolonged tactical fight or an exposed gangway attempt.

## Spatial setup

- **Final ship:** loaded, passengers aboard, still within scheduled departure window.
- **Anna:** at a dockside manifest table immediately outside a heavy stone registry/flood-control building.
- **Tate, Naomi and Liroz:** approaching from the medical loading side; their luggage is already aboard.
- **Zephrine:** on the ship as evacuation security.
- **Flood boats:** stored below the registry building for inspection and maintenance.

## Beat-by-beat order

### 1. Names

Anna marks Tate, Naomi and Liroz present. Several forest-party names remain blank. Her father's line is still unconfirmed.

### 2. First crack

A narrow fracture crosses the sphere. The city does not explode instantly.

Advance warnings:

- pressure in the ears;
- papers lifting;
- lantern flames narrowing;
- rain changing angle;
- Tate's coat pulled sharply toward the harbour.

### 3. Tate acts

Tate understands exposed weather from travel. Anna does not.

Tate orders Naomi and Liroz into the building, then runs back along the shortest line to Anna. She does not overpower the storm or carry Anna across a large distance. She reaches her during the warning interval, grips the back of the coat and torso and pulls her toward the door.

Liroz catches them at the threshold.

### 4. Full failure

The glass sphere breaks. The full wind front arrives only after they are inside.

### 5. Ship swept away

The ship's departure is physical:

- water draws away, then surges;
- mooring posts tear free;
- ropes or emergency fittings break;
- part of the rail is damaged;
- the gangway twists into the water;
- the vessel is pushed sideways into an outgoing current.

Zephrine and Anna see one another through the rain. Neither can cross the distance.

### 6. Laptoon uproots

Golden chains pull through the city:

- pavement bends;
- building foundations lift;
- water pours from raised streets;
- roof lines and gardens tear loose;
- houses expose intact rooms for an instant before breaking apart.

### 7. Monsters enter

Flying creatures appear first through fractures and are thrown or driven into the city. Ground creatures cross the failing boundary. Their arrival turns every exposed street into a closing route.

### 8. Hethrn cutaway

At the tower peak, Hethrn awakens. The manifestation forms close around him rather than filling the entire skyline:

- faded skull;
- elongated skeletal arms;
- arm-ribs two to three times his height;
- hands on shoulders, waist and neck;
- golden chains between wrists and city anchors.

Monsters swarm. Hethrn attacks reflexively. One creature moving toward the registry is destroyed because it enters his reach, not because he has chosen to defend Anna.

### 9. Forest line

Brief intercut:

> **Wulfnoð:** “The prisoner has broken free.”

Edward looks back. The missing party continues toward the unresolved forest threat.

### 10. Flooding building

Water enters the registry. Old flood indicators activate. The building will not hold.

> **Naomi:** “Are there any escape routes?”  
> **Anna:** “Yes.”

### 11. The boats

The group descends to a small maintenance chamber.

> **Liroz:** “Down there?”  
> **Anna:** “Yes.”

Anna scans.

> “Three monsters approaching.”

### 12. Cooperative launch

- Liroz lowers and stabilises the boat.
- Naomi releases the water mechanism.
- Tate watches the stairs.
- Anna unlocks and identifies the correct channel.

No one stops for a full fight. Monster silhouettes reach the chamber as the sluice opens.

### 13. Rain Throat

The boat is taken through:

- steep stone channel;
- old flood gates;
- drops;
- blue-green fungi;
- cracks showing chains above;
- water pressure too strong for free steering.

### 14. Hero conversation

Keep the lore short and incomplete. Anna knows records, not Hethrn's inner experience.

> **Tate:** “That was Hethrn?”  
> **Anna:** “Yes.”  
> **Naomi:** “His power made the barrier?”  
> **Anna:** “They stretched it around the city.”  
> **Liroz:** “The chains?”  
> **Anna:** “He agreed to hold until morning.”  
> **Tate:** “And morning?”  
> **Anna:** “They never gave it back.”

### 15. Outfall

The skiff exits through an ancient carved water-face into the forest river. Anna attempts to look back, but the current takes the group around a bend.

## Character proof contained in the sequence

- **Tate:** reads the outside world and moves first.
- **Anna:** understands Laptoon's systems and opens the route.
- **Naomi:** provides controlled magic and mechanism support.
- **Liroz:** supplies physical force and threshold stability.
- **Hethrn:** kills immediate threats without resuming guardianship.
- **Zephrine:** remains visibly active aboard the ship rather than passively abandoning Anna.

## What the sequence must not add

- a new child the group must carry;
- Anna voluntarily running onto the gangway after the full wind arrives;
- Tate holding back the entire storm with magic;
- a prolonged monster battle inside the registry;
- Hethrn looking down, recognising Anna and deliberately escorting the group;
- a lengthy exposition speech before the boat launches.
