# Comprehensive Episode Treatment

**Working format:** eight connected anime chapters, approximately 7–12 minutes each. The structure can later compress into fewer releases, but the emotional order should remain.

---

# Part I — The City Behind Rain

## Purpose

Make the audience understand Laptoon as a home before asking them to grieve it. Establish Anna, Edward, Zephrine, Tate's expedition, the barrier and Hethrn without explaining every historical system.

## 1. Opening image: ordinary Laptoon

Late afternoon already resembles night. Rain lies across narrow stone streets. Oil lamps glow beneath wet awnings. Fungi grow on selected exterior walls; gardens appear to have been rotated vertically. Roof-line messages travel between homes.

Anna works alone in a small room. In adaptation continuity she has two arms:

- one hand writes an arrival report;
- the other alternates between a calculation sheet and dried fungus;
- her pen taps against the rain's rhythm.

A wet message arrives on the roof line. Anna pulls on her oversized coat and red wolf-emblem hat, then walks toward the main gate.

## 2. Tate's expedition arrives

Two damaged carriages emerge from the forest. The source paperwork identifies a Farvious medical mission; the surviving escort includes Tate, Naomi and Liroz.

Anna performs the gate work she knows well:

- compare faces with sketches;
- check criminal records;
- identify injuries;
- mark seven survivors from fourteen expected;
- arrange medical transport.

She says:

> “Welcome to Laptoon. You are safe now.”

Tate looks back toward the forest before entering.

## 3. The gate incident

Later, a child-shaped traveller appears beyond the gate carrying an adult. The scene preserves psychological horror but reduces graphic aftermath.

Key signs:

- the body is unnaturally cold;
- its reflection does not align;
- a familiar face deforms toward an owl-like structure;
- the figure whispers, “Please…kill me”;
- beyond it stands the Kenwid child: long black hair, white dress, red eyes, three antler-like horns;
- frost moves toward the open gate.

The gate closes. The false bodies collapse into hollow cold forms or brittle material. Edgar takes control; the camera stays with Anna's hands and breathing rather than explicit dismemberment.

## 4. Edward's warmth

Anna returns home unable to articulate what happened. Edward enters from the rain, says his ordinary greeting—“Hey. How are you hanging?”—and prepares the warm milk, egg and sugar drink.

He does not demand a clean report. Anna only begins crying once she is warm and safe.

## 5. Hethrn's garden

At the morning meeting, Queen Anita states that the barrier is weakening. The political discussion remains brief.

Anna later visits the tower roof:

- circular pond;
- stepping stones;
- warm still grass at the centre;
- Hethrn's preserved kneeling body;
- one golden link visible beneath moss.

Hethrn looks like a tired middle-aged doctor, not a pristine legend.

After Anna leaves, the translucent caretaker appears and gently restores his braid. Beneath the pond, a chain shifts.

## 6. The missing investigation

Anita and Edgar investigate the forest, discover evidence that an old restraint has moved, and disappear. Their signal gem begins to crack.

**End image:** ordinary rain falling over Laptoon while something beneath the city tightens.

# Part II — The Last Rain

## Purpose

Destroy a place that has been lived in on screen. Separate Anna from the intended route, awaken Hethrn, establish the travelling group and launch the migration through the city's own water system.

## 1. Four ordinary days

The city continues despite fear. Revisit familiar background residents:

- lantern keeper;
- mushroom seller;
- child using the message line;
- breakfast vendor;
- elderly neighbour.

This ordinary continuation is what gives the evacuation weight.

## 2. The signal breaks

The current king receives Anita's broken signal. He says farewell to his dying father. The king, Edward and a small core party race into the forest.

Emergency bells and drums begin. Zephrine pulls Anna out of shock and into evacuation work.

## 3. Leaving home

Anna and Zephrine check homes and direct residents toward the harbour. Anna initially perceives panic, then notices decades of preparation:

- ordered lines;
- families discussing Bashu;
- people choosing one object;
- doors marked for missing relatives;
- shopkeepers preserving small pieces of living cultures.

At home, Anna finds Edward's note telling her to board the Bashu fleet and register at the Laptoon Reception House in South Harbour.

Her suitcase, money and most equipment are loaded onto the final vessel.

## 4. The final manifest

Anna remains at the dockside manifest table because several names are still blank:

- Edward;
- Wulfnoð;
- Anita;
- Edgar;
- members of the forest party.

Tate, Naomi and Liroz approach from the medical-loading side. Anna marks them present.

The first crack crosses the sky.

## 5. Tate reaches Anna

The crack produces warning signs before the full front arrives:

- ears pop;
- loose pages rise;
- lantern flames narrow;
- Tate's coat snaps toward the harbour.

Tate orders Naomi and Liroz into the nearest heavy registry/flood-control building. Anna remains outside, looking upward and gripping the manifest.

Tate runs back, catches Anna by the coat and torso and pulls her through the doorway. Liroz helps drag them fully inside.

## 6. The sphere breaks

The barrier shatters like glass. The true exterior climate enters:

- horizontal rain;
- pressure drop;
- violent multidirectional wind;
- windows bursting inward;
- message lines tearing loose;
- wall gardens peeling away;
- lanterns extinguishing.

The harbour surges. Mooring posts and fixtures break. The final ship is physically swept into the current with Zephrine and Anna's luggage aboard.

## 7. City and chains

Golden chains erupt beneath roads and foundations. Pavement curves upward. Houses lift, split and scatter. Water pours from raised streets.

Flying and climbing monsters enter through the broken sphere.

## 8. Hethrn

The camera transitions to the tower peak.

The caretaker unties Hethrn's braid. His preserved surface cracks. Golden power contracts from the barrier and forms:

- partial faded skull;
- many elongated skeletal arms;
- arm-ribs around his torso;
- chains through wrists and body.

Monsters swarm him. Hethrn attacks on instinct. He crushes or throws anything entering his immediate space. One creature attacking near Anna's building is destroyed by a passing arm, but Hethrn never looks at the group.

He turns toward the forest.

## 9. The prisoner

Brief cut to the forest party. Golden light rises behind the trees.

The king says:

> “The prisoner has broken free.”

Edward looks back toward Laptoon. The party continues toward the unresolved spear/binding event.

## 10. Water route

Sea water and storm runoff flood the lower floor of the registry building.

Naomi asks:

> “Are there any escape routes?”

Anna recognises the old flood gauge.

> “Yes.”

Below is a narrow maintenance tunnel with flat-bottomed wooden skiffs.

Liroz asks:

> “Down there?”

> “Yes.”

Anna scans upward.

> “Three monsters approaching.”

Liroz lowers the boat. Naomi releases the sluice. Tate covers the stairs. Anna opens the final lock and boards last. The water carries them into the Rain Throat as shadows enter the chamber above.

## 11. Tunnel conversation

Between drops and impacts:

> **Tate:** “The thing at the tower was your hero?”  
> **Anna:** “Hethrn.”  
> **Naomi:** “The barrier was his?”  
> **Anna:** “His power, stretched over the city.”  
> **Liroz:** “And the chains?”  
> **Anna:** “The founders called them an oath.”  
> **Tate:** “Did he?”  
> **Anna:** “The oldest record says he agreed to hold until morning.”

The boat exits through an ancient stone water-face into the forest river.

**End image:** the river carries Anna away while Hethrn walks into the forest.

# Part III — The Wrong Direction

## Purpose

Convert four stranded people into a travelling unit and transform the outside world from pure threat into dangerous possibility.

## 1. River and giant fish

The group drifts downstream in exhausted silence. A spare empty skiff is towed behind them. As the river widens, an enormous one-eyed fish swallows the spare boat in a single movement.

They cut the rope and beach the surviving skiff.

## 2. Hidden passage

Anna remembers a concealed route near the river and finds a crack behind vegetation. Tate's group follows her into an older tunnel.

## 3. Evidence of exclusion

The passage retains the source history without dwelling on gore:

- human-shaped mineral impressions;
- erased names;
- abandoned support devices;
- old children's height marks;
- cloth sealed beneath crystal;
- occasional obscured remains.

Anna explains that an older civilisation hid people it considered defective. The unanswered history remains uncomfortable; the film does not stage a cannibalism lecture.

## 4. Introductions around one lamp

In safer stone, the group finally rests and introduces themselves. Their losses emerge naturally:

- Tate joined the contract at departure;
- Naomi and Liroz knew some expedition members;
- fourteen left;
- only three escorts remain.

Anna shows Edward's Bashu note.

## 5. First sunlight

Tate notices fresh air and tests the exit. Anna hesitates at direct sunlight. Tate pushes her gently through.

Anna experiences:

- dry wind;
- warmth on skin;
- open sky;
- grass and shallow streams;
- fruit trees;
- a waterfall toward the sea.

She smiles for the first time since Laptoon.

## 6. The wrong direction

The map makes the problem clear:

- Bashu is west along the coast;
- Harramius is inland and appears to take Anna farther away;
- no safe coastal route is available from their position;
- Anna has no supplies or recognised travel identity;
- Tate's group knows how to reach Harramius.

Tate's core argument:

> “Farther away on the map is not farther away if it keeps you alive.”

Anna agrees to reach Harramius, recover and choose again.

## 7. First coordinated battle

A large armoured burrowing creature attacks. Each member contributes:

- Liroz stops the charge;
- Anna identifies the underside;
- Tate and Naomi coordinate a projectile;
- the group wins through formation rather than individual dominance.

## 8. Old Sactum

At sunset they climb to a ruined city on a giant stump. It is abandoned, but its gatehouse provides shelter.

**End image:** Anna enters another dead city after losing her living one.

# Part IV — The Giant Road

## Purpose

Give the migration continental scale and deepen the group emotionally before Harramius.

## 1. Naomi's names

At night Anna finds Naomi beneath the moon, quietly repeating the names of those lost. Anna admits she does not know what to say and simply remains beside her.

This is Anna's first attempt to provide the kind of silent presence Edward gave her.

## 2. Giants at sunrise

Cloud-high furred giants cross the plains. Tate calls to one in a language the others do not understand. Rambus approaches and extends a hand.

Tate reveals that Rambus once rescued her when she was stranded.

## 3. World-scale montage

From Rambus's shoulder:

- flying fish;
- golems;
- vast grass patterns;
- distant villages;
- red fields;
- buried stone rings;
- weather changing over enormous distances.

Anna stops trying to catalogue everything and simply watches.

## 4. Harramius appears

The multi-ring city and diverted river become visible. Naomi says, “We are home.”

Anna has arrived somewhere safe, but not somewhere that knows her.

## 5. Separation in the crowd

Naomi and Liroz return to their family. Tate leads Anna through markets and dry heat. The disappearance of the travelling group into ordinary city life triggers Anna's panic.

Tate notices, returns and embraces her.

> “It will be okay.”

## 6. A temporary plan

At Faze's apartment Anna writes letters toward Bashu. Tate explains distance, cost and risk. Anna cannot continue immediately.

A guild licence becomes the practical first step.

**End image:** Anna places Edward's note beside Tate's guild badge.

# Part V — A City Without Rain

## Purpose

Show arrival without belonging. Let Harramius become a lived place through work, routine, economics and relationships.

## 1. Guild registration

Gil receives the expedition report with unsettling efficiency. Anna registers and undergoes a compressed test. She is clever but lacks endurance and receives Class 3 / entry rank.

## 2. Ordinary adaptation

Montage:

- clothes drying rapidly;
- unfamiliar food and market rhythms;
- Anna learning the city rings;
- letters failing to arrive;
- Laptoon fungus being kept alive in shade;
- Edward's field guide used beside new local texts.

## 3. Dungeon entry

Tate takes Anna into the dungeon. The purpose is practical:

- earn money;
- acquire materials;
- qualify for protected travel;
- learn to survive outside Laptoon.

The rule is simple: do not die.

## 4. Knowledge becomes useful

Anna collects plant samples, recognises toxins and adapts Laptoon medicine. Tate reads terrain and monster behaviour. Their first dungeon partnership shows mutual rather than one-way mentorship.

## 5. Economic lesson

The guild's purchase price is poor. Tate demonstrates direct sale to craftspeople and merchants. Anna learns that survival depends on institutions but also on understanding who controls exchange.

## 6. New routine

Faze's food stall and apartment begin to feel familiar. Anna is recognised by shopkeepers. Naomi and Liroz re-enter her week rather than remaining permanent party members.

**End image:** a vendor begins preparing Anna's usual food before she speaks.

# Part VI — Rain Beneath the Earth

## Purpose

Give Anna a visual and emotional answer to Laptoon and make Harramius a genuine home before she chooses to leave.

## 1. Deeper expedition

Anna, Tate, Naomi and Liroz descend with improved equipment and clearer formation. Anna is no longer purely sheltered support.

## 2. Little Blue Tree reveal

A tunnel opens into a cavern more than a kilometre deep:

- inverted tree attached to the ceiling;
- roots large enough to serve as bridges;
- indoor rain beneath a crystal dome;
- bubbles rising from unseen depths;
- clouds within the cavern;
- homes, markets and smithies on the roots.

## 3. Chains and roots

A visual match cut recalls Laptoon's golden chains. The difference is shown through use:

- children cross the root;
- traders carry food;
- homes hang beneath it;
- the structure is alive and not imprisoned.

## 4. Flise and the expanded world

Flise introduces Bashu as a lived place beyond the abstract destination. She can craft Anna's cloak from memory and becomes another practical link westward.

## 5. Rain that does not enclose

Anna eats beneath the indoor rain and admits it reminds her of Laptoon. The resemblance hurts, but does not erase either place.

## 6. Time and competence

Repeated visits show:

- Anna mapping routes;
- making medicine;
- earning trust;
- increasing to Class 4;
- growing Laptoon fungus successfully;
- building enough funds for travel.

## 7. Westbound contract

A protected caravan posts a route toward Bashu's coast. Tate accepts a paid escort contract because the route also leads toward her own northern transfer.

Naomi and Liroz stay in Harramius.

Anna looks back at the city longer than expected.

> “It was not supposed to become home.”  
> “Most homes do not ask permission first.”

# Part VII — The Road West

## Purpose

Make the second migration chosen, show Anna carrying knowledge between communities and restore the intended scale of travel.

## 1. Red grasslands

The caravan crosses red grass beneath enormous swords, armour fragments and unidentified relics embedded in the earth. Travellers use the remains as landmarks.

Anna begins drawing her own route map.

## 2. Tree refuge above monsters

The caravan sleeps in a settlement built high inside a giant dry tree. Large creatures move beneath the roots. The shelter lasts one night.

Anna cannot sleep without rain. Tate taps the old Laptoon rhythm against the floor.

## 3. Sinkhole community

A desert community lives in homes carved around a protected spring. Water contamination has made residents wary of travellers.

Anna contributes rather than merely receives aid. Using Laptoon fungus, dungeon minerals and Edward's field notes, she helps repair the filtration beds over time.

A child asks where she lives.

> “I came from Laptoon. I stayed in Harramius. I am going to Bashu.”

The community repairs her red hat with local cloth.

## 4. Anna leads

A sandstorm erases the normal route. Anna compares buried weapons, wind movement and her maps, then identifies a sheltered passage. Tate accepts her judgement.

## 5. The sea

The group smells salt before seeing water. Bashu appears beyond the final ridge.

**End image:** Anna holds Edward's months-old note against the real coastline.

# Part VIII — The Knock

## Purpose

Complete the migration without pretending that arrival automatically resolves uncertainty.

## 1. Bashu reception

Bashu contains evacuation housing, Laptoon food, adapted wall fungi and records of arriving ships. Zephrine sees Anna and crosses the room to embrace her.

The fleet survived.

Edward's party did not arrive.

## 2. Searching

Anna checks:

- ship lists;
- hospitals;
- guild records;
- churches;
- land caravans;
- missing boards;
- burial reports.

Edward remains unconfirmed.

## 3. Tate leaves

Tate completes her contract and continues toward her own route. Their friendship does not require permanent co-location.

## 4. Waiting differently

At first Anna runs to every ship and caravan. Gradually she begins working at the reception house and living between arrivals.

She uses the same skills she once used at Laptoon's gate to receive displaced people in Bashu.

A frightened traveller reaches her desk.

> “You are safe now.”

The line no longer means “nothing else can happen.” It means “you have somewhere to stand today.”

## 5. A room made from several places

Anna's room contains:

- Laptoon cup and fungus;
- Edward's field guide;
- Harramius guild badge;
- Little Blue Tree cloak;
- desert repair cloth;
- maps drawn on the west road;
- unanswered letters.

She prepares the warm milk, egg and sugar drink.

## 6. The knock

Edward stands outside, injured, exhausted and carrying evidence of the forest journey. He repeats his ordinary greeting:

> “Hey. How are you hanging?”

Anna embraces him, then brings him inside.

The relationship reverses:

- Anna gives Edward the blanket;
- Anna gives him the warm cup;
- Anna sits beside him without demanding the story immediately.

Edward looks around at the room.

> “You made it.”  
> “I kept moving.”

**Final image:** Anna's repaired red hat and Edward's blackened chain fragment rest on the same table while a brief coastal rain falls through sunlight.
