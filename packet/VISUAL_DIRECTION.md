# Visual Direction — After the Last Rain

**Target:** refined, detailed anime fantasy with tactile weather, large environmental scale and emotionally legible character acting.  
**Latest checkpoint:** 2026-08-12

## 1. Novel first, cultural shorthand second

The previous generation pass allowed real-world cultural labels to dominate character identity. Corrective rule:

> Use the manuscript's physical description and the world's climate/function first. Use real-world cultures only as a faint design rhythm.

### Laptoon

Useful influences:

- rain-adapted tailoring;
- layered wool and treated cloth;
- fitted coats, high boots, brass and dark stone;
- formal municipal and church silhouettes;
- restrained wolf symbolism.

Avoid:

- literal Victorian recreation;
- generic gothic aristocracy;
- steampunk machinery pasted onto clothing;
- every person wearing black fur and gold chains;
- making a future-fantasy city look like historical England.

### Harramius

Useful influences:

- heat-adapted layers;
- wrapped sashes and loose trousers;
- open forearms and shoulders;
- bronze geometry, blue stone and warm fabric;
- cloth that can convert into shade or dust protection.

Avoid:

- pharaoh crowns;
- excessive ceremonial collars;
- museum-copy Egyptian jewellery;
- uniform white linen on everyone;
- treating an entire multicultural trade city as one real-world nation.

### Tate

Tate should not inherit Harramius styling merely because she works there. Her white snow-region coat is part of her identity and makes her visually foreign within the desert city.

## 2. Character attractiveness

“Attractively practical” means:

- clear silhouette;
- clothing shaped by movement and climate;
- selective exposure at arms, shoulders, knees or midriff where believable;
- functional belts, wraps, pockets and protection;
- fantasy detail concentrated at focal areas;
- no extreme fan-service;
- no mature sexualisation of Anna or Tate.

Anna is fifteen and Tate approximately sixteen. Their appeal should come from face, expression, motion, contrast and personality.

## 3. Environment register

### Laptoon before the fall

- wet blue-grey stone;
- dark sky at afternoon;
- amber oil light;
- vertical fungi gardens;
- quiet streets that become busy in compact morning periods;
- roof-line message systems;
- sea, cliff, walls and tower always spatially related;
- rain treated as familiar domestic atmosphere.

### Laptoon after the sphere breaks

The change is not simply “more rain.” It is a complete atmospheric violation:

- horizontal water;
- violent pressure;
- snow and ice mixing with rain;
- all flame extinguished;
- loose systems moving at once;
- pavements curving upward over chains;
- houses revealing interiors as they lift;
- sea entering streets.

### Rain Throat

- municipal, ancient and practical;
- dark stone channels;
- bronze mechanisms;
- maintenance skiffs;
- old water marks;
- fungi and mineral glow;
- not a polished theme-park slide;
- speed conveyed through water pressure, impact and limited steering.

### First sunlight

Hold the shot. The environment must not immediately demand action.

- warm exposure on skin;
- dry wind;
- sky without a ceiling;
- shallow water and patterned grass;
- Anna's eyes and hands adapting before the full vista.

### Harramius

- broad commercial roads;
- four historical rings;
- pale warm stone;
- diverted river;
- crowded markets and quieter domestic upper floors;
- many cultures and species;
- heat, dust, food smoke, shade cloth and metalwork;
- practical prosperity beside exploitation and danger.

### Little Blue Tree

- blue crystal light;
- vast inverted living structure;
- roots as roads and civic platforms;
- indoor rain and floating bubbles;
- clouds inside a cavern;
- improvised homes and workshops;
- sense of danger acknowledged rather than sealed outside.

### West road

- red grasslands and relic-scale swords;
- shelters built within temporary geography;
- desert tree refuge above moving monsters;
- sinkhole homes arranged around water;
- repeated evidence that people make ordinary life inside impossible landscapes.

## 4. Hethrn

### Body

- no clean hero armour;
- no idealised youthful face;
- long underfed torso;
- visible tension in hands and shoulders;
- heavy hair and exhausted eyes;
- unexpected muscle as a consequence of strain, not bodybuilding glamour.

### Manifestation

The current preferred image is intimate rather than city-sized:

- faded skull behind head;
- elongated gold skeletal arms two to three times body scale;
- arm arcs read as ribs from a distance;
- hands hold and restrain him;
- some arms attack without his gaze following them;
- gold is weathered and semi-translucent, not polished jewellery;
- chains emerge through both manifestation and city.

The holy/demonic contradiction should remain unresolved. Laptoon called the power sacred because it benefited the city; the form itself was built for war.

## 5. Monsters

Monsters should affect route, weather, architecture and behaviour—not only provide fight shots.

- flying creatures make the ship's separation active;
- creatures entering the registry close off time;
- giant fish ends river travel instantly;
- desert monsters turn a tree into a one-night refuge;
- visual designs can be strange and unsettling without relying on wet gore.

## 6. World-alive rule

Use recurrence rather than more named cast.

For Laptoon, repeat a small set of unnamed residents before and during evacuation:

- lantern keeper;
- mushroom seller;
- roof-line child;
- elderly neighbour;
- breakfast vendor.

For each wide shot:

1. foreground: current emotional characters;
2. middle ground: one ordinary routine;
3. background: one impossible moving feature.

Examples:

- Anna and Edward foreground; neighbour drying cloth; barrier curving behind roofs.
- group on a root bridge; food delivery crossing; bubbles rising through cloud.
- caravan in red grass; traveller repairs a strap; giant sword catches distant light.

## 7. Camera and animation

### Character acting

Prioritise:

- hands;
- hesitation;
- posture before dialogue;
- eye focus;
- fabric and hair reacting to weather;
- delayed emotional release.

### Scale

Reveal scale through interaction:

- building still attached to chain;
- giant hand used as transport;
- root broad enough for a market;
- relic used as a road marker;
- Hethrn's arm crossing a street without filling the whole horizon.

### H3 shot design

- use shots as controlled 4–15 second units;
- anchor first and last frames for transformations and camera transitions;
- maintain character reference packs per costume phase;
- avoid generating long dialogue coverage as one continuous clip;
- let weather, sound and environmental motion carry transitions;
- preserve negative space and stillness around emotional beats.
