# Character Bible — source-grounded adaptation designs

**Rule:** manuscript descriptions are the first authority for physical design. Generated sheets are exploratory evidence, not automatic canon.

## Production age policy

Only Tate is explicitly estimated at around 16 in the source. Anna's exact age is not supplied there; the owner has locked the adaptation at 15. Other ages are production estimates and remain reviewable.

## Anna / Eris Lewine

### Source traits retained

- blue hair;
- dark-green pointed ears;
- emerald/green eyes;
- heavy coat reaching low on the body;
- red hat with wolf emblem;
- notebook, records and biological interests;
- sheltered but highly educated;
- anxious self-hugging and fidgeting;
- practical sword and limited manipulation ability.

### Adaptation locks

- name: **Anna**;
- age: **15**;
- two arms;
- two eyes;
- visually youthful face and proportions;
- no mature glamour treatment.

### Silhouette

- small frame, narrow shoulders;
- oversized weather coat makes her look more protected than powerful;
- red cap remains the immediate identifier;
- satchel, field book and specimen storage matter more than decorative weapons.

### Clothing

Laptoon outfit should be fantasy rainwear rather than literal Victorian dress:

- waxed or mana-treated dark coat;
- short divided lower layer over tights or fitted travel shorts;
- weather boots;
- brass closures and wolf badge;
- pale underlayer visible at collar and cuffs.

Harramius and road clothing can become lighter and more open at forearms and knees while retaining the cap and at least one Laptoon garment.

## Tate / Shirley / Tat

### Source description

- gentle face;
- approximately 16;
- loose grey hair, later explicitly described as long white hair;
- blue water-crystal eyes;
- light/pale clear skin;
- long white coat with gold lining;
- black undershirt;
- one-handed sword at left hip;
- staff;
- Class 5 mage-swordswoman, Bengith / Rank 4.

### Adaptation direction

- no pointed ears unless a later decision adds them;
- northern snow-region origin is current working canon;
- Harramius is a base and network, not birthplace;
- lean conditioned build rather than ornate warrior-princess proportions;
- same coat across climates because it is durable, expensive and personally memorable;
- expression range must include calm alertness, deadpan sarcasm, broad uncontrolled laughter and unexpectedly gentle concern.

## Naomi

### Source description

- Liroz's older sister;
- warm dark-orange/brown skin;
- lighter straw-brown hair;
- styled independent curves at the front, straighter flow behind;
- mage;
- carries the names and grief of expedition members.

### Design target

- young adult rather than royal sorceress;
- layered practical desert fantasy fabrics;
- restrained blue/purple accents;
- staff built for travel;
- jewellery limited to pieces that can survive fieldwork;
- face should remain soft enough for the quiet night-grief scene.

## Liroz

### Source description

- Naomi's younger sister;
- slightly short;
- rounded face;
- dark-orange skin;
- shoulder-length brown curls;
- heavy axe;
- physically strongest member of the immediate group;
- Class 5 fighter, Bengith / Rank 4.

### Design target

- compact, dense muscle rather than great height;
- broad back and powerful legs;
- youthful cheerful face contrasting with strength;
- heavy axe intentionally oversized relative to height;
- practical open-shoulder desert layers with reinforced guards.

## Zephrine

### Source description

- friendly guard and student;
- two goat horns;
- pink curly hair in the primary introduction, although a later line inconsistently calls it green;
- enhanced healing, durability and strength;
- involved in flora expeditions;
- behaves like Anna's sister.

### Adaptation lock

- use pink curls;
- two eyes, with unusual dark irises/highlights rather than the source's four eyes;
- two backward-curving horns;
- athletic, warm and approachable;
- guard equipment should allow rain travel and study work.

## Edward Lewine / Eduart

### Source description

- adviser to king and church;
- medium-length curly dark-blue hair;
- distinctive thick tassel-like curls;
- scattered facial hair;
- slightly bagged eyes;
- large body frame;
- frequently absent due to work;

### Design target

- broad and tired rather than clean romantic lead;

## King Wulfnoð Bluee

### Source description

- large muscular structure;
- sharp eyes with subtle red outline;
- strongly swept-back pointed hair;
- straight goatee;
- broad flattened nose;
- thin disconnected bronze crown;
- two small forehead horns;
- father of a very young child;
- aware that Laptoon may end.

### Design target

- mobile ruler rather than throne-bound ornament;
- crown must not hide the two horns;
- dark tailored fantasy military layers;
- limited fur and bronze chainwork;
- strong enough to plausibly lead the forest party himself.

## Hethrn

### Source description

- preserved body at the tower garden;
- baggy-eyed;
- skinny and tall;
- middle-aged;
- thick long hair;
- mundane, exhausted-doctor face;
- sword planted into the grass;
- associated with quiet presence in nature.

### Adaptation design

- malnourished but sinewy;
- visible ribs/collarbones, strong shoulders and forearms;
- long dark hair with an old braid;
- weathered wraps and torn cloth;
- no pristine armour;
- feet exposed or minimally bound.

### Manifestation

- two to three times his body scale;
- faded partial skull, not always complete;
- eight to twelve elongated golden skeletal arms;
- arms curve around him as a rib cage;
- hands grip shoulder, waist or neck, suggesting protection and imprisonment at once;
- unused hands hover and twitch;
- immediate threats are struck automatically;
- chains remain integrated into wrists, spine and surrounding architecture.

## Anita Teleano

### Source description

- queen of Laptoon;
- strongest fighter since Hethrn;
- blunt and direct;
- three brown eyes in source continuity;
- dog-like ears;
- long dark hair, later inconsistently described as brown during the expedition;
- glass-flower jewellery;
- red pearl chain joining the ears behind the head;
- switches to simple blue gems and removes elaborate accessories for field work.

### Adaptation choice still open

The generated sheet preserves three eyes. If facial simplicity is applied consistently, reduce to two eyes and retain unusual sight through markings or magic. Do not decide silently.

## Hethrn's caretaker

Source form has many fading limbs and quietly braids Hethrn's hair. The adaptation may either retain an abstract many-limbed translucent body or simplify it into a pale veil-like spirit with strands that behave as hands. Its function is more important than a species explanation: it is the only presence still treating Hethrn as a person.
