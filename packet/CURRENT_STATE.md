# After the Last Rain — Current Development State

**Checkpoint:** 2026-08-12  
**Continuity:** parallel anime adaptation of `Side story.pdf`  
**Game canon:** unchanged

This is the short operational snapshot for future sessions. The longer documents remain the detailed authorities.

## Locked adaptation decisions

- Anna is the adaptation name for source Eris and is **15**.
- Anna has **two arms and two eyes**. The source's original four-arm/four-eye anatomy is not used for the animation.
- The story is a migration story first: Laptoon falls, Anna is displaced, she travels with Tate/Naomi/Liroz, builds a temporary life in Harramius, then continues to Bashu.
- Multiple episodes/parts are expected; the story should not be compressed just to hit a ten-minute limit.
- The fake-father / thirteen-hearts plot is removed.
- Gore is reduced in favour of psychology, scale, implication and environmental horror.

## Core character corrections from the latest design pass

### Anna

The current Anna design is the strongest visual anchor so far. Keep the youthful 15-year-old proportions, blue hair, green/emerald eyes, dark green pointed ears, red wolf-emblem hat and oversized weather clothing. Fashion remains a live art pass, but it should stay fantasy-first and practical.

### Tate

Return to the manuscript rather than the earlier desert/Egyptian reinterpretation. Tate/Shirley/Tat is approximately 16, pale/light skinned, with loose grey-to-white/long white hair, blue crystal-like eyes, a long white coat with gold lining, black undershirt, one-handed sword and staff. Current working continuity places her origin in a northern snow region; Harramius is her adventuring base, not her required birthplace.

### Naomi and Liroz

They are Harramius sisters. Naomi is the older mage with warm dark-orange/brown skin and lighter straw-brown hair. Liroz is the slightly short younger sister with a rounded face, dark-orange skin, shoulder-length brown curls and a disproportionately large axe. Liroz's strength comes from compact physical power rather than height.

### Hethrn

Do not use the old city-sized skeletal giant as the default. Current design: an exhausted, underfed but sinewy middle-aged man with long hair, surrounded by many elongated golden skeletal arms roughly two to three times his size. The arms curve like a rib cage, sometimes holding his shoulders/waist/neck, while other arms hover or attack. A faded partial skull may appear behind him. His golden power should read simultaneously holy and demonic.

## Laptoon collapse is now a fixed sequence

1. Anna completes the final manifest while several people remain unaccounted for.
2. Tate, Naomi and Liroz approach; their luggage is already aboard.
3. The sphere cracks like glass.
4. Tate recognises the atmospheric danger and gets Naomi/Liroz into the nearby stone registry/flood-control building.
5. Tate runs back and pulls Anna inside before the main wind front arrives; Anna does not run onto the gangway.
6. The ship is physically swept away by wind, surge and broken moorings. Zephrine remains aboard.
7. The city begins to uproot as monsters enter, chains tear through roads and houses split apart.
8. Cut to Hethrn at the tower peak as his guardian arms manifest and monsters swarm him.
9. A brief cut to the king's party establishes that the prisoner has broken free and the forest investigation continues.
10. Floodwater enters the registry building.
11. Anna identifies the old escape route: a maintenance tunnel containing small wooden boats.
12. Her scan finds three approaching monsters; the four launch immediately.
13. The group travels through the Rain Throat and exits through the ancient carved water-face into the forest river.

## Route

**Laptoon → Rain Throat → forest river → ancient passage → first sunlight → Old Sactum → giant road/Rambus → Harramius → Little Blue Tree → westbound caravan → red grasslands → tree refuge → desert sinkhole community → Bashu → reunion.**

The exact final ordering of some western travel scenes remains reviewable, but this is the current intended scale.

## Location visual canon

### Laptoon

Rain-soaked cliff/sea city near the forest. Dark afternoon skies, oil lanterns, wet stone, moss and fungi growing on building faces, narrow alleys, roof-line message systems, giant enclosing walls, a circular plan and the Tower of Gods on a steep hill near the sea. The city should feel lived-in and warm before it falls.

### Tower of Gods / Hethrn's roof garden

Largest structure in Laptoon, slightly off-centre toward the sea, on a steep hill surrounded by walls and a small village. The upper roof becomes a circular stone pond with loose bronze root-like cagework and stepping stones. In the centre is a grassy island holding the kneeling hero statue with his rusted sword. Within the garden the wind becomes strangely calm and the temperature warmer.

### Forest of Keltons

A dangerous forest region where plants appear white and cotton-like because of dense webs from highly poisonous creatures. The party uses masks. The visual should be beautiful at first glance and deeply unsafe on inspection.

### Old Sactum

Ruined city occupying the top of a giant tree stump. Access is by a narrow road/stair carved around the trunk and takes roughly half an hour to climb. At sunset the party reaches a worn stone castle with broken walls and an old gatehouse that can provide one night's shelter.

### Blue Stone Water Face

A large carved woman's face built from stone on brown rocky steps beside a river. It is tinted blue and partly covered by dark blue-green vegetation. Moss colours the face. Empty eyes, nose and slightly open mouth leak bubbling green-blue water matching the river. The area is filled with mellow blue mist caused by algae in the water. This is the preferred Rain Throat outfall in the adaptation.

### Harramius

A large inland trade city shaped by mineral processing and surrounded by sand. The source describes four historical rings, a diverted river, broad commercial streets, dense markets, an open view toward the sea, and a mix of crowded urban cores and crumbling older outskirts. The city should feel multicultural and practical, with subtle desert fantasy influence rather than direct Egyptian costuming.

### Little Blue Tree

A gigantic underground cavern more than a kilometre deep. An enormous upside-down tree is attached to the ceiling; roots stretch across the void and become bridges and civic platforms. A crystal-like dome provides blue light and indoor rain. Transparent bubbles rise from the murky depths. Buildings, markets, smithies and homes occupy platforms around the tree. This is one of the strongest visual anchors of the adaptation.

### Kameripa

A forest mountain settlement built from stone near brown rocky slopes and surrounded by wooden fences. Its people are heavier-furred, broader and shorter than humans, with strong jaws, tougher bodies and a preference for mixed/raw food. Their architecture and tools should feel grown or tied together with roots and vines rather than refined urban masonry.

### Western travel environments

The current visual exploration covers red grasslands containing enormous relic-scale swords and objects; a tree refuge above moving desert monsters; and a sand-hole/sinkhole community built around scarce water. These are migration environments, not isolated spectacle scenes: people repair clothing, trade food, maintain shelters and continue ordinary routines inside impossible landscapes.
